Don't stay alone with it

Reaching out, made easier.

The hardest part is often the first message. O₂ helps with that, and with the moments you don't want to talk at all.

Reach someone

Pick what would help: just be with me, listen, or take my mind off it. Your message is written for you. Change anything, then send it from your own phone.

"Hey, something hard happened. Could you just listen for a bit? You don't have to fix anything."

Help me say it

Apologize, say you're not ready to talk yet, ask for help, or set a boundary. Fill in a few blanks and your words come together. No AI, nothing sent until you send it.

"Hey Sam, I'm sorry I snapped at you earlier. You don't have to answer right away."

Sit with me

Don't want to talk? Two quiet minutes with a calm, breathing presence. Invite a friend with a link and see "Patrick is here" when they join. No chat, just company.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Anyone can open O₂ Rhythm on their phone and use every tool for free.

Do I need to download anything or sign up?

No. It opens in your phone's browser. There's no account, no email, and no password.

Can I put it on my phone like an app?

Yes. Open O₂ Rhythm on your phone and tap "Add O₂ to your home screen." On iPhone: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android: tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app. It gets its own icon and opens in one tap.

If I invite a friend to sit with me, what do they see?

A short note that someone asked them to sit for two minutes, then the same calm screen you see. No chat, no messages, no names stored. They just show up, and you see that they're there.

Who can see what I write?

If you keep it private, nobody. It stays on your phone. If you choose to leave something for others, it's reviewed first and shared without your name.

Is this therapy?

No. O₂ Rhythm is for the minutes after a hard moment. It works alongside counseling, peer support, and crisis lines, not instead of them.

What if I'm in crisis?

Please reach out now. Call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline) or call 911 if you're in immediate danger.

Can my school, team, or workplace use it?

Yes. See organization access for QR codes and ready-made materials.
