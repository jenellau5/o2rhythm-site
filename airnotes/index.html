Open Air Notes See How It Works

A simple place to start

Big feelings don't always come with the right words.

Air Notes gives kids and teens a low-pressure way to take a minute—without needing to explain everything first.

Pause

Take a breath or a short break when a moment feels like a lot.

Write

Put the thought somewhere, even if it isn't perfectly said.

Read

Find a kind note that makes the moment feel less lonely.

For adults supporting them

A small next step can help.

Air Notes is a supportive resource, not therapy, medical care, or an emergency service. If a child may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or an appropriate crisis resource.
