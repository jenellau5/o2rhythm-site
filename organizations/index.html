How it works

Place it. Scan it. Do one useful thing.

01

Place your QR code

Break rooms, consoles, locker rooms, waiting areas, badge cards. Wherever hard moments happen.

02

They scan

O₂ opens right to their moment. No app, login, or explanation.

03

One useful thing

A one to three minute reset: breathing, grounding, or a quick physical release.

04

What next

They say how they feel now. Better: they get back to work. Worse: they see your support contacts and an easy way to reach someone.

Privacy is the advantage
